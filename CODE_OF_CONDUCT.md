# Code of Conduct

We pledge to make participation welcoming and harassment-free regardless of identity, background, experience, or ability. Be respectful, assume good intent, give actionable feedback, protect private information, and do not pressure anyone to engage with game prompts they do not want.

Unacceptable conduct includes harassment, threats, sexualized attention, doxxing, sustained disruption, or publishing another person’s private information. Maintainers may edit, remove, or reject contributions and temporarily or permanently ban participants when needed to protect the community.

Report conduct concerns privately through the repository owner’s GitHub profile. Reports will be reviewed promptly and kept confidential as practical.
