# Privacy policy

Effective: August 26, 2026

Pocket Party Games does not create accounts, run analytics, show ads, contact a hosted backend, or sell/share personal information. Player names, preferences, onboarding state, and Solo Timer statistics are stored locally on the device and can be reset in Settings. Active games and nearby rooms are not restored after the app closes.

Nearby play uses the local Wi-Fi network only. A host phone temporarily advertises a room and exchanges encrypted game state directly with approved participant phones. Room tokens, private roles, prompts, drawings, votes, local addresses, and device identifiers are not sent to us or retained in cloud storage. Rooms end when the host closes them or the disconnect grace period expires.

Camera permission is used only to scan a room QR code. Local Network/nearby-service permissions find and host rooms. Wake-lock keeps timed rounds visible. Sound/haptic features use device output only. Permissions can be denied; manual joins remain available where possible.

The app includes user-directed social and physical party challenges. The Bold category may contain mature or sensitive prompts. Content is bundled with the app; it is not generated or moderated online. Players control participation and should skip any prompt that is unsafe, illegal, unwanted, or inappropriate for their group.

Questions or deletion requests do not require contacting us: use Settings → Reset all local data or uninstall the app. Security reports should follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).
